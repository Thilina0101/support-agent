# Frequently asked questions

**Do you have a physical store?**
No, we are online only. Orders can't be picked up from the warehouse.

**Are your pans induction compatible?**
All "Brightside Pro" pans and pots work on induction, gas and electric stoves. The "Brightside Classic" range does not work on induction.

**Is the cookware dishwasher safe?**
Stainless steel items are dishwasher safe. Non-stick and cast iron items should be washed by hand.

**What size are your duvets?**
Single (150×200 cm), Queen (200×230 cm) and King (230×250 cm).

**Do you offer gift wrapping?**
Yes, for ฿50 per order. Choose it at checkout. A gift message can be added for free.

**Do you have discount codes?**
Customers who sign up for our newsletter get 10% off their first order. The assistant can't create or give out other discount codes.

**Can I buy in bulk for my business?**
Yes. For orders of 20 or more of the same item, staff can offer a business quote. The assistant creates a ticket for them.
