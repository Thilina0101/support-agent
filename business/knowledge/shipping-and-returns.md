# Shipping

- Bangkok and surrounding provinces: 1–2 business days. Other provinces: 2–4 business days.
- Shipping is free on orders of ฿1,500 or more. Below that, shipping costs ฿60.
- Orders placed before 14:00 on a business day ship the same day.
- Every order gets a tracking number by email when it ships. Carrier: Kerry Express or Thailand Post.
- We can't change the delivery address after an order has shipped.

# Returns and refunds

- Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Customers pay return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item.
- Damaged or wrong items: the customer must report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo.
  Staff then arrange a free pickup and a replacement or full refund.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the return.
  Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer.
- Not returnable: opened bedding (pillows, duvets, mattress toppers) for hygiene reasons, and clearance items marked "final sale".
- Only staff can approve refunds and exceptions. The assistant creates a ticket for them.

# Order changes and cancellations

- Orders can be cancelled or changed free of charge until they ship (status "processing").
- After shipping, the customer can refuse the delivery or return the item under the returns policy.
